        How scoring works

        Three scores, one weighted total. Every point comes from something the lead actually said — the evidence is listed under each score.

        Lead score = Budget × 40% + Urgency × 30% + Fit × 30%

        70+ Hot — ready to buy, reply within the hour.   45–69 Warm — interested, reply today and ask for budget and timeline.   Under 45 Cold — one polite reply, then move on.

        Budget — 40%

        A stated amount, the word "budget", or "I'm ready to pay" pushes it up. Asking your rates counts too — it shows buying intent. "Free", "exposure" or "no budget" pulls it down hard.

        Urgency — 30%

        "Today", "ASAP", "this week" or a launch deadline pushes it up. A named start date (even next month) still counts. "No rush" or "just exploring" pulls it down.

        Fit — 30%

        Matches against the services actually offered: websites, online stores, social media, WhatsApp automation, SEO, data, transcripts, proposals. Logo, video or photo work is out of scope and pulls it down.
